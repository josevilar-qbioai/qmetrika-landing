Flagship line · L2

Therapeutic mRNA optimization

mRNA sequence design from the nearest-neighbor stacking (ΔG) thermodynamic axis — a physical dimension that LinearDesign and secondary-structure energy (MFE) don't capture. The dual optimizer improves both at once.

evidence

The dual optimizer (ΔG + MFE) simultaneously beats BNT162b2 and mRNA-1273 on both metrics; the stacking axis carries a dimension of its own — 78 % of its variance is neither local composition nor secondary structure (13 sequences, nucleotide resolution) — information current methods don't use. With RNA parameters the design also lands at mRNA-1273-level CpG (o/e 0.70), which matters for immunogenicity.

Method covered by patent P202630522; the optimizer is proprietary technology. Open to collaboration and licensing with mRNA pharma/biotech.
